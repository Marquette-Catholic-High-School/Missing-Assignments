Missing Assignment Slips

Upload a spreadsheet of missing assignments as a CSV file, or paste a link to a Google Sheet. A printable PDF of slips, one per student, will download to your computer.

Columns needed: name, teacher, assignment. Download a sample file.
Same three columns as the CSV. The first tab is used unless the link points to another tab.
Printed in the sentence at the bottom of each slip.
Page layout

Preparing the spreadsheet

In Excel or Google Sheets, make a sheet with three columns and one row per missing assignment. For a CSV, use File → Save As (Excel) or File → Download → CSV (Sheets). For a Google Sheet, paste its address above; any sheet you can open in Google Sheets will work.

name,teacher,assignment
Jane Doe,Mr. Smith,Chapter 4 Vocabulary Worksheet
John Public,Mrs. Johnson,Lab Report
  • Column headings can be in any order. “Student Name”, “Name of Teacher”, and “Missing Assignment” are also recognized.
  • Extra columns are ignored. Rows with no student name are skipped.
  • Long entries are shrunk to fit on the line.

Printing

Print at 100% scale (not “fit to page”) so the slips come out the correct size. The dashed lines mark where to cut.
